# 2001 Military Personnel Context Alternatives

This table records the best available numeric context for the 11 roster IDs that lack a comparable active-personnel source row. `source_date` is the cited document's publication or legal-source date; `context_period` describes the population or event period and can differ. The table keeps the game's factual active-personnel series unchanged. No row is eligible to replace that series because the entries use different scopes, observation periods, or evidence types.

The 2001 Afghanistan scenario estimate is reproducible from the published faction ranges: Taliban fighters 40,000–45,000, including 8,000–12,000 foreign fighters, and Northern Alliance forces 22,000–25,000. Midpoint arithmetic gives `(42,500 - 10,000) + 23,500 = 56,000` Afghan-origin faction participants. This is a low-confidence conflict-participant estimate, not a state military count.

Other numeric entries are bounded alternatives rather than active-force counts:

- Kosovo's 3,000 is the Kosovo Protection Corps active establishment ceiling; the Corps was a civilian emergency service.
- Monaco's 112 is the 2002 Carabiniers du Prince headcount, one year after the scenario. It excludes other public-force bodies.
- Montenegro's 15,000 is the reported 2000–2001 territorial presence of 14,000 Yugoslav troops plus 1,000 military police. It is a federal force stationed in the territory, not a national Montenegrin army.
- Palestine's 30,000 is an authorized maximum for Palestinian Authority police. The actual number was not declared.
- Somalia's 10,000 is an approximate registration pool of former soldiers and recruits, not an active-force headcount.
- Zero values for Antarctica, Puerto Rico, and Solomon Islands are narrow scenario scope proxies: respectively, no sovereign Antarctic force, no separate Puerto Rico national force, and no regular Solomon Islands armed force. They do not claim no military or security personnel were physically present.
- San Marino and South Sudan remain without numeric alternatives because the reviewed sources do not provide a compatible 2001 strength. San Marino's sources identify two small units without headcounts; South Sudan was not a sovereign state in 2001 and SPLA estimates describe a rebel organization.

All values are read-only context. The full source notes, dates, limitations, and eligibility flags are in [military_personnel_context_alternatives_2001.tsv](military_personnel_context_alternatives_2001.tsv). A separate [gameplay-only estimate ledger](military_personnel_gameplay_estimates_2001.md) models nine scenario values from 2001 population and regional WDI service-rate medians; those estimates are explicitly not historical observations and do not reuse the contextual numbers above. The exact active-personnel source gaps remain listed in [military_personnel_gap_audit_2001.md](military_personnel_gap_audit_2001.md).
