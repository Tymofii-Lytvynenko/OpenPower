# Palestinian natural-reserves observations, 2000–2001

The candidate ledger contains two distinct PCBS observations for Palestinian natural reserves:

- The 2000 Statistical Abstract directly reports 54.4 km² across 19 natural reserves. The table presents this as the Palestinian Territory total, with all 19 reserves in the West Bank and none listed in Gaza.
- The 2001–2002 PCBS press release says the prior-year figure was unchanged and reports approximately 54 km² across 19 West Bank natural reserves.

PCBS footnotes that its natural-reserve data cover only reserves controlled by the Palestinian National Authority (PNA). Wadi Gaza was separately declared a nature reserve in 2000, while the PCBS table lists no Gaza reserves. These records therefore preserve the PNA-reported natural-reserve observation and its scope; they do not establish a complete inventory of protected areas across all authorities or designations.

Keep the measure separate from national parks: the sources classify the sites as natural reserves and provide no national-parks-only total. Keep it separate from the broader 2000 UNSD terrestrial protected-area percentage: the PCBS numerator is authority-limited and does not establish the full all-category area. The existing PSE broad protected-area and park fields remain unavailable.

Sources:

- [PCBS, Land Use Statistics in the Palestinian Territory 1999–2000](https://www.pcbs.gov.ps/Downloads/book850.pdf), Table 14 (p. 60), scope footnote (p. 61). The abstract directly reports 54.4 km² and 19 reserves for 2000.
- [PCBS, Land Use Statistics in the Palestinian Territory, 2001–2002](https://www.pcbs.gov.ps/Portals/_pcbs/PressRelease/lus_e.html), paragraph on natural reserves. It reports that approximately 54 km² across 19 West Bank reserves was unchanged from the previous year.
- [UNESCO World Heritage Centre, Wadi Gaza Coastal Wetlands](https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/5722/), which reports that Wadi Gaza was declared a nature reserve in June 2000.
- [PCBS, Area of Land by Type of Use and Governorate, 2000](https://www.pcbs.gov.ps/Downloads/book837.pdf), Table 3.1.1, which reports 6,020 km² for the Palestinian Territory. This denominator is not used to convert the PNA-limited natural-reserves observation into the broad protected-area field.
