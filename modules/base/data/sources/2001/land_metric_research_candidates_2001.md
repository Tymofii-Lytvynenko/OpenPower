# Additional land-metric source review for the 2001 scenario

This review extends the existing FAOSTAT and Montenegro/Kosovo candidate notes. Four 2001 CIA World Factbook edition values are now imported with their observation year explicitly unknown: Monaco arable share, Vatican City arable and forest/woodland shares, and Taiwan land area. A blank `observation_year` means the source edition is dated 2001 but the source does not state when the underlying estimate was measured. Other rows remain comparison-only or unresolved. The complete row-level evidence and current-field status are in [`land_metric_research_candidates_2001.tsv`](land_metric_research_candidates_2001.tsv).

## Imported 2001-edition records with unstated observation year

The 2001 CIA World Factbook gives a source-compatible Taiwan area tuple: total 35,980 km², land 32,260 km², water 3,720 km². The table already contains the CIA total and water values; only land is blank. The archived CIA edition is available through [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/27638) and the field transcription is [Taiwan, 2001 edition](https://thefactbook.org/taiwan/2001). The source does not state an observation year, so preserve that as unknown. Do not mix this value with the official DGBAS figure of 36,188.04 km² or the FAOSTAT land-area figure of 35,410 km²; those are different area bases and cannot be reconciled with the current CIA total/water pair without replacing existing data.

The same CIA 2001 edition explicitly reports Monaco arable land as 0%, and Vatican City arable land and forests/woodland as 0%. These values are now loaded from [`factbook_land_metric_supplement_2001.tsv`](factbook_land_metric_supplement_2001.tsv), with the source publication year retained as 2001 and `observation_year` left null. The archived source text is [`factbook.txt`](factbook.txt); the primary 2001 edition is available at [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/27638/pg27638-images.html). Monaco forest is already populated as 0; Monaco and Vatican FAOSTAT `other_land_pct` values are already populated and are not replaced by the Factbook's 100% urban estimate.

## Exact-year evidence held out by definition or scope

The DGBAS *Statistical Yearbook of the Republic of China 2020* reports a 2001 Taiwan land area of 36,188.04 km² and forest-land area of 2,101,719 ha. Their arithmetic ratio is 58.0777%. This exact-year national observation is now imported as a source-labeled alternative because the harmonized WDI/FAOSTAT forest field was blank. “Forest land area” is not established as the FAO/World Bank forest-area definition, and its denominator conflicts with the CIA area tuple; the historical provenance and UI note retain those caveats. The source is the official [DGBAS yearbook](https://ws.dgbas.gov.tw/001/Upload/466/ebook/ebook_90655/pdf/full.pdf), table “Land, agricultural land and forest land areas.” The 2001 CIA Factbook's 55% “forests and woodland” and 24% arable shares remain comparison values, not replacements for exact-year Taiwan observations.

The official *Statistical Yearbook of Yugoslavia 2002* contains a section titled “National Parks, 2001.” Under the Republic of Montenegro it lists Biogradska gora 5,400 ha, Durmitor 39,000 ha, Lovćen 6,400 ha, and the Lake of Skadar basin 40,000 ha, for a total of 90,800 ha (908 km²). The same yearbook reports the Montenegro part of Lake Skadar at 369.7 km². The park table does not give the water/land split, so the evidence ledger records an explicitly labeled lower-bound inference: subtract the full lake surface from the Skadar park, then add the other parks, yielding 538.3 km². Dividing by FAO FRA's 2000 land area gives 4.0022%. The result is not a complete park inventory and is not presented as a direct observation of park land. Source: [Federal Statistical Office of Yugoslavia, Statistical Yearbook 2002](https://pod2.stat.gov.rs/ObjavljenePublikacije/G2002/pdf/G20022003.pdf).

The same 2001 national-parks table lists Serbia's Derdap, Tara, Kopaonik, Fruška Gora, and Sharr/Shar Planina parks, totaling 1,596 km². Sharr is the Kosovo park already represented on the KOS row (390 km²). The Yugoslav yearbook's territorial table reports Serbia at 88,361 km² and Kosovo and Metohia at 10,887 km², citing the Federal Geodetic Administration (1953). The current SRB historical row is 88,360 km², so its territorial scope includes Kosovo. Importing the Serbia park sum and retaining Kosovo separately would double-count Sharr; neither park aggregate is recommended until the project chooses one consistent Serbia/Kosovo mapping. The yearbook explicitly cautions that most 1999–2001 statistics omit Kosovo, so each individual table's territorial scope must be checked; the national-parks table itself places Sharr under Serbia.

### Entity and source limits

- **Antarctica (ATA):** no sovereign country-level land-use denominator or national park belongs to a state row. Treaty-system protected areas are not country-level national parks.
- **Kosovo (KOS) and Serbia (SRB):** the 2001 yearbook gives 10,887 km² for Kosovo and 88,361 km² for Serbia including Kosovo; current SRB is 88,360 km² and therefore overlaps KOS. FAOSTAT's 2001 land-use country is “Serbia and Montenegro” (M49 891), so its arable, forest, land, and other-land numerators cannot be split into SRB, KOS, and MNE from that dataset.
- **Montenegro (MNE):** 2000 FAO FRA geography is already populated with its observation year retained. Existing notes preserve the 2001 Monstat arable candidate separately because “plough-land and gardens” has not been proved identical to FAOSTAT arable land. No comparable other-land residual is added.
- **Nauru (NRU) and Tuvalu (TUV):** their CIA 2001-edition land-use vectors are explicitly based on 1993 estimates. For TUV, the current 2001 FAOSTAT forest share is already 34.3333%; the CIA zero is comparison-only, not a replacement. NRU forest 0 and other land 80 are also already populated from 2001 series.
- **South Sudan (SSD):** the 2001 territory was part of Sudan (former), M49 736. The table's Sudan observation includes present-day South Sudan; duplicating it into SSD would assert an unsupported territorial backcast.
- **Vatican City (VAT) and Monaco (MCO):** their CIA 2001-edition arable zeroes and Vatican forest/woodland zero are loaded as edition-context values with null observation years. They do not replace populated FAOSTAT `other_land_pct` values.
- **Taiwan (TWN):** the CIA 2001 land area is loaded with the existing CIA total/water tuple. Exact-year DGBAS forest land is now shown as a national-definition alternative; the CIA 55% forest/woodland figure remains excluded because it has no stated observation year. Taiwan's exact-year FAOSTAT arable value remains primary.
