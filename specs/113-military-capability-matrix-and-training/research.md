# Research: Military Capability Matrix and Training

## Decision: Keep role data and capability policy in the existing module layer

- **Decision**: Continue using `modules/base/systems/military/capability_catalog.py` for canonical role profiles, capability bounds, and bounded training/research projections. Keep lifecycle decisions in `MilitaryCapabilitySystem`; keep the existing tactical resolver authoritative for round and battle lifecycle.
- **Rationale**: This matches the current module registration and state-table architecture and avoids duplicating research progression or combat lifecycle.
- **Alternatives considered**: Move role rules into `src/shared` or `src/core`; rejected because those layers must remain policy-free.

## Decision: Reuse persisted intelligence findings as authorized observations

- **Decision**: Derive foreign capability/force estimates only from military espionage findings and treaty-authorized allied sharing. Expose them through a passive military presenter view model with source, observation time, confidence, freshness, and explicit estimated/unknown status. Never send the target's authoritative unit rows to the viewer.
- **Rationale**: `CovertOperationsSystem._intelligence_snapshot` already creates persistent military findings and incorporates source telecom and assigned-cell coverage into confidence; `MilitaryPresenter.covert_findings_for_country` already applies ally-sharing authorization and freshness. The remaining work is to include bounded composition estimates and make the best eligible estimate visible in the military UI.
- **Alternatives considered**: Read foreign `units` in the client to build a live force list; rejected because it leaks authoritative data and turns the passive client into an intelligence authority.

## Decision: Treat readiness increases as bounded covert observations

- **Decision**: Convert upward `EventUnitReadinessStarted` transitions into deterministic `military_mobilization` findings only for foreign cells assigned to espionage, present in the target country, and communications-ready. Persist only domain and posture transition; never persist a unit or source-cell identifier in the public observation. Reuse the existing owner/ally authorization and receiving-country confidence cap.
- **Rationale**: The readiness system already publishes a durable event contract before the posture transition completes. This makes the signal a report of mobilization intent rather than a claim that the unit has already completed its transition, and keeps intelligence policy inside the covert module.
- **Alternatives considered**: Expose readiness state directly to every country or add a new event channel visible to clients; rejected because either bypasses intelligence authorization.

## Decision: Make training eligibility explicit in persisted state

- **Decision**: Represent eligible training sites and instructor/capacity constraints with scenario/mod-configurable module state. A training order records its assigned site, training location, return location, total cost, start/completion/cancellation time, last event, progress, and state; unavailable facilities or instructors pause without erasing progress, while a new order is rejected if no eligible slot or funds exist.
- **Rationale**: The current training system treated any deployed unit region as a training facility and had no instructor/capacity data, despite the specification's explicit location and instructor edge cases. The module registry/schema boundary supports a reusable table-driven contract without embedding scenario policy in core or client. Requiring a compatible site at the unit's current region avoids introducing a parallel movement policy for a unit that leaves the map during training.
- **Alternatives considered**: Treat every region as an unlimited training site; rejected because it cannot represent unavailable-location or instructor edge cases. Moving units to remote training sites was deferred because it would duplicate route/access decisions already owned by military movement.

## Decision: Preserve data-oriented state and measure before optimizing

- **Decision**: Keep Polars frames authoritative and make capability projection updates revision/change-aware. Establish a deterministic 50,000-unit/10,000-design fixture that changes 5% of records and separately reports setup, projection, and write time.
- **Rationale**: The current projection builder materializes and evaluates every unit on each update. SC-007 requires incremental behavior, so a deterministic isolated 50,000-unit benchmark establishes a comparable baseline before any algorithm change.
- **Alternatives considered**: Weaken the 1-second threshold or run the benchmark concurrently with unrelated heavy processes; rejected because neither demonstrates the specified gate.
- **Optimization budget**: At most three measured algorithm-optimization iterations for SC-007. Record every attempt and comparable result; after iteration three, leave the issue open and move to other functional specifications if the target is still missed.

## Audit evidence

- The current catalog defines default land, air, naval, and strategic profiles and bounded training/research transforms in `capability_catalog.py`.
- `MilitaryCapabilitySystem` handles training actions, costs, pause/cancel, off-map lifecycle, and combat-facing projection. Training orders now persist their site, training location, lifecycle timestamps, and event; the registered facility table provides domain, instructor, capacity, and enabled-state checks. `_build_projections` currently re-evaluates every unit; the measured baseline is recorded below before the change-scoped cache implementation.
- AI procurement now filters designs through the same research-requirement policy used by the authoritative production system. AI training candidates share the same facility-selection and cost functions as human training actions, exclude human-controlled countries unless authority is delegated through the existing scheduler, and submit `ActionTrainUnit` to the existing authoritative handler. Training proposals are generated only when funding, deployment, battle participation, domain, instructor, and capacity checks allow them.
- Unmeasured scaling risks for later resolution: `MilitaryTrainingPolicy` currently materializes and sorts the unit table once per daily decision to find one eligible unit per AI country, and AI procurement materializes design prerequisite metadata on its decision cadence. These are not benchmark evidence or observed slowdowns; profile them with normal AI workloads before considering optimization.
